When siding is worth a look

  • Boards that are cracked, warped, soft or pulling away from the wall.
  • Paint that will not stay on, or stains that come back after every winter.
  • Gaps around windows and doors where water can get behind the wall.
  • An older house where you want one material that is easier to keep up.

What we do

We replace siding on houses across Roseville and the Sacramento Valley. We work with fiber cement siding, including James Hardie.

A new skin only lasts if the layers under it are right. So we check the wall under the old siding, the water barrier and the trim around windows and doors, and we write down what we find.

What the estimate tells you

  • Which walls we replace, and with what material.
  • What happens to trim, windows and doors along the way.
  • What is included and what is not, in writing.
