Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how West Coast KBP handles information when you use our website or contact us about a construction project or estimate through our website or Facebook and Instagram forms.

Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry, we collect the information you choose to provide, including:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your ZIP code.
  • Your answers about your project, such as the type of work (siding, windows, remodeling, or an ADU) and when you would like to start.

How we use your information

We use your inquiry information to contact you about your project, discuss your needs, and respond to your request for an estimate.

We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and tracking

Our website uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand website activity. They use cookies and similar technologies and receive information such as page views and clicks on our phone and email links, along with device and browser information. Meta receives these events through the Pixel.

You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. You can manage Meta's use of information for ads in Meta ad settings, and block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Browser controls may also limit these tools.

Requesting deletion

You may ask us to delete the information you have provided by emailing owner@westcoastkbp.com.

Contact us

For questions about this policy or your information, contact West Coast KBP at owner@westcoastkbp.com.
