Product direction

Keep the context. Carry the work forward.

We are designing the assistant to connect each source, confirmed fact and next action across conversations.

Remember

Keep the source beside what you confirm, so a later conversation can pick up the matter.

Follow through

Keep the next action and its observed result with the task.

Stay in control

See what is happening and review actions that need your decision.

These are design goals. Each workflow needs a released version and a demonstrated result.

Proposed workflow · illustrative

A letter you can return to.

A photographed letter is the first personal workflow we are developing. This sequence illustrates the intended experience.

  1. Photograph

    Capture the letter from your phone or tablet.

  2. Understand

    Get an explanation linked to the document.

  3. Confirm

    Review the important facts before they are saved.

  4. Keep

    Save the confirmed information with the matter.

  5. Return

    Find it in a later conversation.

The proposed first version excludes payments, sending and deletion.
