When windows are worth a look

  • Fog or moisture between the panes.
  • Drafts, rattling, or a sash that will not open or lock.
  • Soft or stained wood around the frame or under the sill.
  • Rooms that get too hot in a Sacramento summer.

What we do

We replace windows in houses across Roseville and the Sacramento Valley. A window is only as good as the opening it sits in, so we look at the frame, the sill and the flashing around it, not only the glass.

If we replace siding on the same wall, we do the windows and the wall together, so the seal around each window is done once and done right.

What the estimate tells you

  • Which windows we replace, and what type and size.
  • What happens to the trim and the wall around each opening.
  • What is included and what is not, in writing.
